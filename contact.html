Frequently asked questions

Is this an official website?
No — this is a fan project made for an HTML & CSS learning assignment, not an official site.
Where can I watch Attack on Titan?
The series is available on several major streaming platforms; availability depends on your region.
Do I need to read the manga to understand the anime?
No — the anime covers the full story on its own, though the manga has some extra detail in a few scenes.
