Why I love Attack on Titan

I built this site around my favorite anime — Attack on Titan (Shingeki no Kyojin). The project was made for an HTML & CSS basics assignment, but every piece of content on it is genuinely about the show.

Why I picked this topic

This series stands out for how it weaves humanity's fight for freedom, deeply layered characters, and atmospheric animation into one story. I prefer watching it with the original Japanese dub, since I feel the voice actors' emotion comes through exactly as intended that way.

Here are a few reasons I keep coming back to it:

  • A complex, multi-layered plot structure
  • Real political and philosophical depth
  • High-quality Titan battles and ODM gear action scenes
  • Long-term, believable character development

How to use this site

Use the navigation above to read more about the series (the "About the Anime" page) or to check the season comparison table and leave feedback (the "Table & Contact" page).

Here's a suggested order for exploring the site:

  1. Start here on the home page for an introduction to the show
  2. Visit "About the Anime" for the story and key facts
  3. Check the season table and leave a comment on the last page
